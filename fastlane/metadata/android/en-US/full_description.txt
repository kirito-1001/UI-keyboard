<p><i>UI Keyboard</i> is a free, open-source keyboard for Android with Arabic and English support. It is built on top of FlorisBoard and developed by Khaled Yagami.</p>
<p><b>Features:</b></p>
<ul>
    <li>Arabic and English layouts, with quick language switching from the toolbar</li>
    <li>Word suggestions and next-word prediction in Arabic and English (turn them on in Settings, Typing)</li>
    <li>Learns from your typing; the data stays on your phone only and can be cleared at any time</li>
    <li>Quick toolbar: clipboard and history, undo / redo, emoji, text editing, voice input</li>
    <li>Ready-made themes and your own image as the keyboard background</li>
    <li>Custom keyboard font (ttf / otf), adjustable key corner roundness</li>
    <li>Key customization with reset options</li>
    <li>Incognito mode</li>
</ul>
<p>Word suggestions use the FrequencyWords lists (CC BY-SA 4.0). UI Keyboard is released under the Apache License 2.0.</p>
